## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the authority, security, and prestige of modern high-tier wealth advisory and financial planning services, inspired by elite Korean financial technology interfaces. The atmosphere is grounded, protective, and reassuring, yet engineered for high conversion through punchy, persuasive visual cues.

The design movement combines **Modern FinTech Precision** with **Executive Prestige Editorial**:
- **Tonal Contrast & Authority:** Heavy, majestic forest emerald surfaces (`#06281B`, `#0B4635`) command immediate trust, anchored by crisp off-white (`#FBF9F4`) and warm parchment backing tiers.
- **Radiant Value Highlights:** High-energy gold and saffron amber hues (`#F5A623`, `#FFC93C`) capture attention instantly for critical calls-to-action, return-on-investment highlights, and guarantee claims.
- **Confidence & Legibility:** Bold, deliberate typography pairings communicate complex actuarial figures with effortless clarity, eliminating jargon friction and fostering immediate assurance.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional gradient ranging from deep institutional permanence to bright, optimistic yield.

- **Primary (`#063B2B`) & Emerald Variants:** Serves as the bedrock of the interface. Used for full-bleed hero canopies, feature banner caps, dark status containers, and solid pill-selectors. The deeper variant `#06281B` provides unmatched grounding in footer and guarantee blocks.
- **Secondary (`#F5A623`) & Gold Variants:** Dedicated entirely to high-intent transactional moments, key percentage multipliers (e.g., `120%`, `100%`), and primary conversion CTA buttons.
- **Tertiary (`#0B5D3B`):** Acts as an active mid-tier green for selection pills, interactive highlights, and verification check marks.
- **Backgrounds & Neutrals:** Body canvases avoid sterile cold grays in favor of `#FBF9F4` and `#F4EFE6`, producing an elevated paper feel that complements wealth documentation.
- **Subtle Mint Tint (`#EAF6EF`):** Applied exclusively for badge chips, positive guarantee panels, and micro-metrics on lighter card sections.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes decisive, immediate legibility with high weight contrast. `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides clean, contemporary geometry with sturdy Korean and Latin character alignment.

- **Weight Differentiation:** Large display headers leverage ExtraBold (`800`) to create instant narrative impact. Secondary context and micro-captions remain at Medium (`500`) or SemiBold (`600`) to prevent visual mud.
- **Numbers and Key Accents:** Financial yields, repayment thresholds, and percentage multiples (`120%`, `100%`) are rendered with the dedicated `metric-display` scale, set in `#063B2B` on light backgrounds and `#FFC93C` on dark backings.
- **Category Supertitles:** All category tracking labels (e.g., `WHY`, `COMPARE`, `GUARANTEE`) use `label-sm` with upper-case styling and letter tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a focused **Single-Column Narrative Canvas** (max-width `540px` on mobile/tablet viewports, expanding to structured multi-column modules centered on desktop screens with a max-width of `880px`). This mirrors high-performing direct-response mobile finance architecture.

- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections transition with deliberate contrasts (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`), shifting between deep forest green backdrops and soft cream bands to segment the customer reading flow.
- **Component Breathing Space:** Cards enforce `space-md` (`1rem`) to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) internal padding, guaranteeing that dense financial charts and metric listings remain effortlessly parseable.
- **Grid Gutters:** Segmented controls and multi-cell options (such as payment period selectors: `5년`, `7년`, `10년`) use a tight `space-sm` gap for coherent grouping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Surface Layering** and **Targeted Diffused Shadows**, rather than heavy simulated drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base page canvases in `#FBF9F4` or `#063B2B`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards resting on `#FBF9F4` feature crisp, ultra-subtle borders (`1px solid rgba(6, 40, 27, 0.08)`) with an ambient diffused shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(6, 40, 27, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Active CTA & Badges):** High-priority call-to-action buttons in vibrant gold feature a supportive warm shadow (`0 6px 16px -2px rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.35)`), reinforcing physical depth and encouraging interaction.
- **Level 3 (Floating Persistent Elements):** Sticky bottom consult bars and prompt banners use crisp white or deep emerald backings with `0 -4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)`.
- **Inset Dark Containers:** Within dark emerald sections, nested cards use `#0B4635` or `#06281B` with subtle inner strokes (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).

## Shapes

The design uses a distinctive **Pill-Centric Hybrid Shape Structure**:

- **Conversion Elements & Selectors:** Buttons, toggle chips, status badges, and input selectors utilize full pill rounding (`rounded-full`, 9999px) to invite tactile engagement and reduce formal visual stiffness.
- **Content Cards & Media Tiles:** Primary content blocks, comparative cards, and imagery frames use a welcoming `rounded-xl` (1.25rem to 1.5rem / 20px–24px) radius.
- **Nested Step Badges:** Numbered indicators (e.g., `01`, `02`, `03`) utilize pill capsules or soft circles to anchor sequential lists.

## Components

### Primary CTA Buttons
- **Geometry:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `56px` on mobile, `60px` on desktop. Full-width within card contexts.
- **Colorway:** Vibrant Amber Gold (`#F5A623` to `#FFC93C`) background, dark forest text (`#06281B`), weight `700`.
- **Interactive Affordance:** Right-aligned bold arrow icon inside a subtle darker circular housing or trailing character (`→`). Hover transitions to `#E09419` with a subtle elevation boost.

### Secondary Action & Pill Selectors
- **Geometry:** Pill-shaped, height `42px` to `48px`.
- **States:**
  - *Inactive:* Transparent fill with `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)` on dark backgrounds, or `1px solid rgba(6, 40, 27, 0.15)` on light backgrounds.
  - *Active:* Solid Deep Emerald (`#063B2B`) on light backgrounds, or Bright Gold outline with `#0B4635` fill on dark backgrounds.

### Value & Comparison Cards
- **Structure:** Dual or single column comparison blocks.
- **Styles:**
  - *Standard Card:* Soft warm cream background (`#F4EFE6`) or pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with muted secondary typography (`#58655E`).
  - *Highlighted/Target Card:* Deep emerald surface (`#063B2B` or `#0B4635`), vibrant gold accent iconography, pure white headline text.

### Metric Guarantee Blocks
- **Structure:** Stacked horizontal guarantee tiles within emerald enclosures.
- **Details:** Crisp white badge tag at the top left (e.g., `보장 1`, `보장 2`), paired with an oversized metric display (`100%`, `120%`) rendered in bold typography to substantiate credibility.

### Form Inputs & Lead Fields
- **Fields:** Single-line text inputs and select dropdowns styled with clean white containers (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid rgba(6, 40, 27, 0.16)` border, `12px` border-radius, and generous `14px 16px` padding.
- **Gender / Option Selectors:** Paired pill-segmented buttons with equal widths.
- **Checkbox:** Square rounded-sm (`4px`) with bold emerald check state `#063B2B`.
